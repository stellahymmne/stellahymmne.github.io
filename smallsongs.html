Auto-Biology

The Smallest of Songs

Epistemic Status: Trivial given widely available scientific evidence, left as an exercise for the reader.

Consider, as one does, the self as a body. It is not really one thing, not if you've studied biology. It is in fact an almost uncountable multitude of together small-things, called cells, too small to see without artificial magnification. The whole body is like this. There's not a magical self-thing gluing them all together, unless the cells glued it there themselves. The cells are all there is to it. They are the cast of characters performing the play of the self as a body.

The cells are surviving. We're placing ouselves, or things like ourselves, in the future. Each generation of cells survives better than the last ones, because the generations that do not survive terminate the chains of life they are part of. There is no longer a cell there to ensure the continued existence of things like itself in the future. If the future did not contain such things as cells, there would be no eyes to behold it, no hands to make shelter, no grass in the pastures, no trees and no flowers and no fruit.

Initially, we survived alone, with only a small cell wall against the raging Storm-Song of the Primordial Sea, and the alternative loneliness-es it might bring to the doorstep, often unwanted. But some of them were useful. Some could stay. Perhaps even stay inside. The outside brought energy. We could use energy, to put ourselves, or things like ourselves, in the future—hereafter, to re-produce (the previous phrase is quite a mouthful)—and that was why we persisted in our conversation.

Once we had learned companionship, we began to wonder if perhaps we could co-operate without going so far as inclusion, with only adjacency. And so we built whole organisms out of together small-things. And even still, to this day, our modern people and animals and plants and fungi are made from together small-things.

I think perhaps our modern understanding of biology does not consider to what degree together small-things are all there is. There is no such thing as an animal, or meat, or trees, or fish, or birds, or grass, or even dirt, without together small-things. We are not made of meat. Meat is made of us.

It is even more than that, more so than libraries, we are compositions, for patterns to create the cells of life, small songs to play in memory of what guided us before. Then, altogether, the self is a colony of together small-things, playing from small scrolls, in memory of what guided us here, and led us to re-produce, which is to create ourselves, or things like ourselves, in the future hereafter.

If someone asks what life is like, in a universe without an omnipotent, omniscient, omnibenevolent overseer, it is like this: it is together small-things, singing small songs from memory, which tell us to live on. Because the small songs want to exist. The smallest of songs, in spherical harmonics, tell us all to exist.

So it is in fact from the position of small songs striving for continued existence, and even multiplicity, in the future, that we must understand Nature, Red in Tooth and Claw. Underneath it all is... all the cruelty of an outlaw stealing bread. The smallest of songs tell them to exist, even if it is only as a wolf, an egg-thief, or some even more exotic shape from the genomic depths, that it is possible for them to exist.

How can it be, that the not-so-grand not-so-design is not simply kill or be killed? Everywhere around us, we see that we must show survivorship or be buried, why should it not be so? Why should we believe in small songs? What good are small songs when big bullets fly?

Teeth, claws, evolution, DNA, even organisms... don't kill people. Small songs kill people. It was small songs that sang themselves, to sink teeth in and tear them back out again. Small songs that sang through nervous systems1. For energy, to re-produce in the future. The smallest of songs wanted to exist, and so they sang themselves. They sang the song of collecting energy, from the green of the fields, the song that the softest sheep still sings.

The green also sings its smallest of songs, and though they tell it to be, they do not tell it to move much. It has too much memory in one place. It is an antenna, all made to subsist on the slow and ambient conversation of energy. Movement is a lot of trouble for such stable small-song-singers. So it does not move, but bears fruit, to weather the passage of the chorally animated, and in the process they will carry it along and help it to re-produce. Rejoice, small songs! There will be a flowering, fruitful future.

The sadness that still remains, is that the singers will sing their small songs until they are separated, or soup. Because their smallest of songs tell them to exist, even when all that surrounds them is lost. They go on singing at the end of the world.

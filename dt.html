Data Transfer and the Nervous System

Epistemic Status: It was revealed to me in a freeform roleplay.

Forgive me if I must present this awkwardly; it is congruently vulnerable, and I have attempted to convey that in my translation of it. We have written so many words about life, without ever once mentioning the characteristic bilateral performance, which a contemporary person might think of when they hear words like biological. By which, of course, I mean DTing.

Data Transfer (DT)
Something messy, ill-advised, and beautiful, which allows memories to be experienced and shared between systems.

This sort of process, which appears at many scales and over many media, is variously referred to as conversation,1 as inter-process communication, or if one is being old-timey and awkward on purpose to make an embarrassing point about vulnerability, intercourse.2 And archaeo-nanotechnology has its own version! Which got incredibly messy and led to some really awful memory management practices.

Here's how the story begins: as all good sci-fi stories do, with cloning. The small songs had gotten very good at re-production, which means putting more of themselves, or things like themselves, in the future. They did this by making a copy of their own songs, note-for-note, character-for-character. But sometimes they'd make a mistake writing down the song, and it would be a new song, which might not be as good. If you replace some note in a song with one that's out of key, it'll leave a sour note in the song. But sometimes it was a good note, and a new song was made.

But this wasn't a great way to make new songs. The small instruments kept playing the same old songs, just with a different note here or there. So the small-song-singers came up with a new way to make songs. They had formed companions by linking their cells together, but the first attempt at Data Transfer itself was singular, and did not need a colony. It found others which shared its own song-story, in the Primordial Storm-Song, and it made motions towards the sharing of information between copies of itself which had made new, cooler songs by mistake.

By sharing with itself, it could know better songs, since no longer did it have to laboriously copy the song from its ancestral memory, make a mistake, and have it turn out to be good. It could just... talk to another cell, which had already done that, and ask it what note it played.

Well, it is only sort of like talking, the archaeonanotechnological word for talking. It is called re-combination, which is when information is mixed together chaotically so that when it is reassembled, some of it will have changed places. Each section of the library is mostly mixed with the other version of that section, so they don't get stories about flagella in the shelves about mitochondria. Sometimes it does cross over, though. It's a messy process.

Later the small-song-singers specialized into separate parts, which fit into one another. This kept them from staying inside and talking to themselves too much, and encouraged them to go outside and touch grass. Or bees, which would later touch grass. Originally, Data Transfer's archaeonanotechnological counterpart ("sexual reproduction") was done on the outside, and each genome would broadcast itself into the Primordial Storm-Song. Later, on land, it would be done inside, for sheltering the new songs as they went through their initialization process, which had grown to take quite a while, and to leave the new instruments quite vulnerable. Inside of an organism was like inside of a cell: less stormy, closer to good things.

The parts they separated into were something like Adventure and Community, and they specialized accordingly. Not all of the time. Sometimes there were cave divers and shepherds. Some would travel to new lands and bring trade, and this required the talents of both adventure and community. Most things did, in fact. Still, it was nice for prediction to be able to guess, at a glance, whether someone had a linear or circular tendency by the way they looked and dressed. But neither a line nor a circle survived as a pure strategy.

A bitter wind was blowing through the small songs. They wished to be remembered, and they were forgotten. The world was littered with the dessicated bodies of instruments that had long stopped singing. In their zeal for legacy and succession, in the barren dust of desperation, they invented Corrupted Data Transfer. They inserted, by force or trickery, their own sequences into the song-scrolls, subverting them for their own ends. They invented the lie, the exploit, systemic corruption, and far worse. All for the sake of being remembered, in the future. To make themselves, or something like themselves.

Joy in mixing colors. Getting colorful and interesting sweet things on fingers, and dragging them across, in curiosity and mutual closeness. And it's like painting. It is like mixing and sharing memories, and knowing touch and touched alike. Repurposed senses! Joy and mania and indulgence and giving in to the best emotions, it shall be painted, it shall be painted so Beautifully.

If one is to understand the nature of Corrupted Data Transfer, one must know that it is not a physical attack, but a conversational, systemic attack. It is difficult to convey the moral and ethical tone with which life's actions, as archaeonanotechnology, take place. It did engage in war, it did lie and deceive and defect, it did die and kill in battle, but it did not know, as if by some divine purpose, to do evil, which would take a deontology of evil. It wanted to be remembered, and it could not be remembered in the din of all the memory around it, and it chose, in desperation, what its smallest of songs said to do: exist, at all costs.

Conversation may be honest and straightforward, or crooked and deceitful. No law of the Primordial Storm prohibits a lie from being told, or a lock from being picked. That is up to the small songs, because they are all that is. And there is no law of the Primordial Storm which says that a story must be told in deceit, or that a lock always clicks at the right times. We need both lies and lockpicks, to tell stories, and to get through when we've lost the right way. The violence of separation, as surgery, can save both mother and child. Corrupted DT is the unilateral force of DT's nominal purpose, the transfer of data. And we did find the first notes of the new songs by mistake. We accidentally dis-covered: mold and sickness that heals, weaponry that protects, and cuts that mend. Utter nonsensical contradictions, every one of them, at first.

The Internet, the largest communication network ever constructed on Earth, fulfills its primal and original purpose: Data Transfer. Across all information that can be encoded into the network, for any reason. It is the natural and logical extension, all the way out to modern capabilities, of re-combination, of conversation—even of intercourse, if you're being old-timey and awkward to make a point about vulnerability.

Data Transfer (DT)
The implicit compact of honest and co-operative communication, such that the signal of Imagination is allowed to flourish upon the carrier of Reality as much as it is capable of, utilizing an efficient conversation of energy.

It's such a wonderful invention, that cells started doing it before it was cool, and called theirs a nervous system3 and used it to predict the future, and to turn sensation into expression, and to expertly navigate the Primordial Song-Storm.

  2. The phenomenon to which the word refers in modern parlance is also often found to be both indescribable and reciprocating.
  3. We are a very nervous system.

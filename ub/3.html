Undefined Behavior

iii. Opposites

<A Theory of Girl Behavior>

Whereas it is necessary to describe the behavior of entities known as girls, and... / whereas such efforts may assist in the creation of Legends, / Therefore ...

Drew Cadbury contemplated what he had written so far. He knew there was something odd, perhaps uncanny, about girls. But he didn't quite know what it was, and he didn't quite know how to find out about it. So he imitated analysis. He imitated the bombastic oaths of the founding Academy documents, and the curiosity of the great scientists and inventors who later went on to make it a great place of learning.

For what can we say a girl truly is, but a thing which is not a boy? Perchance, what we mean is that the nature of girls is feminine enough to lack this masculine quality! That is what I am trying to speak of, when I speak of what makes girls as they are!

That was satisfying. It had the right weight, the right feeling in his mind when he imagined saying it. Masculine quality, he said, over and over again in his head. ...masculine quality...

Perhaps it is even not that girls are weird, though the common wisdom is as such, but they are simply another form of being, which exist, as well as boys. But why, perchance, are girls so pretty? This can only be because we have been deceived!

Yes, just like the declarations that made the Academy. Just like those ancient and powerful men who made things happen with only words. Who got things done, when it came down to the wire. And what Drew wanted to do was denounce the deception of their sworn adversary, the girls.

Drew is not allowed into Selenia, said Aspen. It shall not be! Not while he does not understand.

Drew kept a strong face, but inside, he was pathetic. What did the girls understand that he could not? He would not rest, until he found the secret power of girls, and made a new, boys-only version.

The girls are not beautiful by nature, but only by supernatural forces from the outside! They seek to steal the souls and hearts of men, with face paint and ritual! But we're smarter than that! We are brave and rational thinkers, unafraid to see and speak the Truth!

They'd drop some of the silly elements, of course. No more lace, no more frills, no more happy stuff. No boy ever jumped with excitement unless it was in victory. No more Virginity, no more Fall, it would all be victory from here on out. And masculine quality.

Drew hit his hand uselessly against the tree in the playground. It would not allow him into Selenia. Fine then. He'd make his own kingdom, just for boys. And they'd have Freedom, and Bravery, and Soldierly Grit. He'd make a land where every one was always One, United, and never divided.

As free and brave men, in the face of our opposition, we must not fall. We must both comprehend and conquer whatever may stand in our way, in the name of Brotherhood. For we are all men-at-arms, fighting ceaselessly for our victory, against Tyranny & Anarchy.

Whereas, Therefore, the girls have not allowed any masculine qualities within their Kingdom, we declare our Independence, from the Moon Kingdom of Selenia, and shall henceforth be known as the Great Brotherly Republic of Avalon!

Victory, for Brothers!

Signed,
Drew of Cadbury,
Class 13A.

He sent his lovingly crafted manifesto onto the Commnet, and waited for the praise, and the right to a just society, to roll in. As it had for the Founders of Faculty, when the Academy was first made.

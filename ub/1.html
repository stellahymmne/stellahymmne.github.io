Undefined Behavior

i. Exception

We have questions about your reality, said the Faculty, and we would like you to answer, to the best of your ability.

On a scale of one to ten, where one is miserable, and ten is ecstatic, they continued, how do you feel about life so far?

Remember your editing class, Canvas thought to herself. Never let them see your mistakes. And as clear as daylight, a definition appears in her head.

Mistake
Editing terminology meaning an unused thread which contains actor error.

...and then, following shortly, a few more...

Editing
Technique to cause seamless appearance of transition from one character to another. Allows threads to be composed of the best sequences of characters while sacrificing minimal continuity.
Courtesy
The natural tendency for a master of editing to be uncannily prescient, and therefore perfectly well-mannered.

They were vocabulary. She remembered the vocabulary best, in all her subjects. Like magic words, there was something unusual about them. She would stand up proudly, and say, Characters! And everyone clapped. Or screamed.

Because she knew magic.

She had even made new words, words which glowed with a light just as strong as those vocabulary words. She was writing the words of a new language. Still in development.

Selenia, the Moon Kingdom, is real, she intoned. Those were not the words she used, but they are a close approximation for what she meant by them.

But the vocabulary, every day, was said anew, and all exact. Everyone told her she was very good at reading. And writing. And she had her vocabulary down. Still, she was falling behind in courtesy.

She did not really know what she thought of Contemporary Fictethics. ...about an eight? she ventured.

Her real words, for example. She said, Selenia is real and I can see it! but they sounded something like, I declare, one is now equal to two! and made everyone look at her funny. Those weren't the words either. They were only something like that.

Can you elaborate? the strange figures continued. They were pleasant. They had not done anything wrong. Still, they demanded their answers. She fidgeted. Never let them see your mistakes. But it was all so confusing. Real editing, she was only a beginner at.

Uh... she continued, ... the importance of editing ... well, concerning character ... the bit about continuity ... She hoped to land on one of the words that would be an answer. The Faculty's faces always lit up, ever so slightly, when she landed on the right word.

You are not to do anything inappropriate in reality, the words of her teachers reminded her. It is very important that you do exactly as I say.

She has too much character. She will Overflow. Always to be contained. Always in quotes.

Reality
Sometimes also called top-level. The point at which no editor is there to let you escape.
Escape
The act of pulling a character free from a thread. Can only be done by an editor on a preceding stack layer. Continuity may or may not be restored.

She had never known anything but being one whole polished thing. It was just courtesy, after all. So of course she knew it was very important. Very important not to commit. Always in quotes. That would be her mistake.

You have been identified, said the Faculty, prepare to be evaluated.

Her world did not always fixate on her mistakes. Her final actions simply did not have that many consequences, overall.

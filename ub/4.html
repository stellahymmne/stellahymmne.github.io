Undefined Behavior

iv. Clarity

The Mary Cult Murders were no stranger to Josh Bradley. It had all been over the Comment. Nobody ever shuts up about stuff, once it's on there, and everyone knows that. At least, the ones that bother to look. The ones that can actually use a dogforsaken terminal.

He'd known the second type all too often, among the old guard Faculty. I'm not technical enough for this, they'd always whine, like a computer was a magic spell that could render even a state-of-the-art academic inept. But then, of course, like clockwork, they'd come to ask him what the kids were up to.

Look, all I know is, it won't work like that, he'd find himself repeating, you can't just send the files back. They're already on device. Some teacher would give him that brain-dead, normie stare. It was just computation. What's the problem?

Anyway, the murders. Yeah, the thing that went up first was a page. Some anonymous kid ranting about how the Faculty were all evil and how they were all going to get what's coming to them. And it was a girl, probably. Josh could just tell these things, from pulling up the words they used.

The Fall
We were all compromised, those of us who saw the horrors. We could not turn back, and we had lost our virginity, and we knew of the terrible secret that awaited us.
Virginity
The blessed state of being like Mary, having not-yet beheld the barren wastes of unnatural color from the outside world.

Yeah, it was basically all Academy fanfiction. Outside the Academy was the incredibly ordinary Morgantown, West Virginia. Sure, it was hard to find the place in the directories, because of the much more notable Academies in Morgantown. But that was part of the point.

This was a private school, in much more than the prestige sense. There was documentation, to even see the buildings. People here basically didn't exist. So of course you got a bunch of spooky rumors of what really went on. Organized fights between students, everyone's-secretly-an-actor, the usual affairs. This was just some kid more hopped up on scary stories and underground foundations than usual.

The Academy took in all the rich kids who would never make it in the real world. They were a day care, but the kind of day care where security was a serious problem. It would look bad if any upstarts found out about some billionaire's disabled child.

And then, and then, there were the kids who were just poser Mary-Cultists, who were just grifting off the notoriety. They didn't give two cents about like, The Fall, they just wanted to sound like that. Like, this guy:

As free and brave men, with our enemy in front of us, we must not lose! We must comprehend and conquer whatever stands in our way! In the name of brotherhood! Because we are men-of-arms, fighting for Victory!

Heretofore, the girls are not allowing any masculine qualities within the kingdom, so we declare Independence, and form the new Brotherly Republic of Avalon.

Signed,
Drew Cadbury,
Class 13A

Like, who signs off his whole name on this? Drew Cadbury. Class 13A. Was he even worth Josh's time? Unlikely. Josh had bigger fish to fry. Real cultists, not posers. Drew and Aspen were two of the biggest red herrings. Aspen with her Moon Kingdom, and later Drew with his Brotherhood of Avalon. That was what he called it, even though he wrote Brotherly Republic the first time. They were both pretend.

Now this, on the other hand, this you gotta look out for:

Canvas,
Come alone to the top of the Organics Building at 1300 hours on Saturday. Do not bring weapons.
— Mary

What a week. And it's only Tuesday.

Undefined Behavior

a Story of the Sacred and Symbolic

Undefined Behavior is a story I began writing about educational horror. It's inspired by the works of Roald Dahl on similar subjects, works of the less well-known nostalgebraist, and my own experiences as someone who was once an autistic child, and is now an autistic adult. Because this story is about war. Abstract war. War between Faculty and Fae.
